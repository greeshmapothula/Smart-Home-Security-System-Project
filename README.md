# Smart-Home-Security-System-Project

Engineered end-to-end product strategy and supply chain models for a smart home security solution, combining high-level competitive positioning with rigorous engineering and operational execution. On the strategy and design side, conducted building automation market analysis to define a differentiation strategy, reverse-engineering industry leaders to pinpoint performance gaps, translated customer needs into actionable engineering specifications and selected optimal hardware architecture. Concurrently, built quantitative supply chain models and inventory management to optimize replenishment cycles and manage portfolio cannibalization. At the end, constructed a comprehensive financial model to identify key margin drivers and streamline subsystem costs.
